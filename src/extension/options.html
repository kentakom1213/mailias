Step 5 of 7

Configure the Worker

  1. Select mailias in Workers & Pages.
  2. Open Settings → Variables and Secrets.
  3. MAILIAS_SECRET · Secret: key from your password manager.
  4. MY_ADDRESS · Variable: destination email address.
  5. Select Deploy.

Step 6 of 7

Connect Email Routing

  1. Open Email Routing for the root domain containing your mail domain.
  2. Destination Addresses: add MY_ADDRESS and verify it via the confirmation email.
  3. For subdomains: add one in Settings → Subdomains and wait until ready.
  4. Enable Routing Rules → Catch-all for the mail domain.
  5. Choose Send to a Worker → mailias and save.
Open Email Routing
