Step 4 of 7

Back up the recovery key

Save this key in your password manager before continuing．After this step，mailias stores only a non-exportable key in the extension．

This is the only time mailias will show the raw recovery key．

Create a password-manager entry now．You will retrieve this key again in the next step and paste it into Cloudflare．

Step 5 of 7

Configure the Worker

Open your Worker in Cloudflare and add the two runtime bindings below．Retrieving the recovery key from your password manager here also confirms that your backup works．

  1. Open Workers & Pages and select your mailias Worker．
  2. Open Settings → Variables and Secrets．
  3. Add MAILIAS_SECRET as a Secret using the recovery key from your password manager．
  4. Add FORWARD_TO with the verified destination email address．
  5. Select Deploy in Cloudflare．

Step 6 of 7

Connect Email Routing

In Cloudflare Email Routing，send the catch-all address for your mail domain to the mailias Worker．This routing rule is not visible through /health，so confirm it here after saving the rule．

  1. Open Email Routing for your mail domain．
  2. Set the catch-all action to Send to a Worker．
  3. Select your mailias Worker and save the rule．
Open Email Routing
